Draft Recaps 2025? How Value is calculated
Value = a player's actual season points minus the points scored by whoever finished at the position rank matching where they were drafted.

Positions are ranked separately from each other (WR vs. WR, RB vs. RB, etc.) using both draft order and final season points.
Ex: Drafted as the 4th RB off the board, but finished as the actual #1 RB (416.6 pts). The real RB4 finisher scored 366.3 pts.
Value = 416.6 − 366.3 = +50.3

Pos rank and draft pick are both position-relative (e.g. WR4 = 4th-best scoring WR). Value compares actual points to what a typical player taken at that draft slot scored.

Manager Efficiency ? How Efficiency is calculated
Each week, we compare the points a manager's starting lineup actually scored against the highest-scoring lineup they COULD have started, given their full roster (starters + bench) and the league's position requirements.

Efficiency = Actual Points ÷ Optimal Points, averaged across every week.
100% would mean a manager started their best possible lineup every single week — no player left on the bench who outscored a starter.

Ranked by efficiency — how close each manager came to their maximum possible score every week, not by who won the most.
